The EduSavvy Multi-Agent Learning Ecosystem demonstrates agent orchestration using RANK, HUNT, GUARD, TRAIL, PRIDE, and CYCLE frameworks. Student questions are processed through specialized agents responsible for curriculum identification, cultural localization, language adaptation, ethical governance, and collaborative learning support. Data is stored through Google Sheets to support memory management and auditability, while PRIDE review points and GUARD safety rails ensure transparency, fairness, and educational quality
